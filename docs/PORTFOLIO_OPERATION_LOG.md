## P03 policy amendment — 2026-10-20

The portfolio owner replaced the 28-calendar-day observation with a seven-day accelerated check of the central dashboard's scheduled refresh, validation, and Pages deployment path. The existing two daily schedule observations predate this amendment and remain historical only. The new clock starts at the first successful natural scheduled run on `main` after the four-hour cadence is merged.

- Cadence: `17 */4 * * *`, yielding 42 expected slots over 168 elapsed hours.
- Closeout requires at least 40/42 slots (≥95%) with successful validation and deployment, at least one complete success on each UTC day, no two consecutive failed/missing slots, and documented cause/disposition for every miss or failure.
- Runs triggered by push, pull request, or manual dispatch do not count. Wait for all expected runs in the seven-day interval to finish before closeout.
- The dashboard refresh reads published evidence; it does not execute the five framework test suites. Report this only as dashboard scheduling/refresh/deployment reliability, not as framework-test stability or month-long operation.
- Verify run event types, exact timestamps, conclusions, deployment results, and the public dashboard snapshot independently at closeout.
